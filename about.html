Our story

We gave construction an identity as considered as the structures it delivers.

ABSTRUCT started on a simple complaint: buildings were being sold on renders and delivered on excuses. We set up to do it the other way round, with fewer promises, written down, and kept.

Today we are site managers, engineers and cost planners working across residential, commercial and project management briefs. The structure is the same on every job: a named lead, a weekly look-ahead, and nothing hidden from the client.
